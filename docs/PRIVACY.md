# Privacy Policy — AI Drive Assistant

_Last updated: [FILL IN DATE]_

AI Drive Assistant ("the App") is developed by comptaflow ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is used.

## 1. On-device processing only

The App performs all AI processing — object detection, collision-risk estimation, driver-monitoring (drowsiness/distraction detection), and lane-drift estimation — **locally on your device**. No camera frames, audio, or AI-processed data are transmitted to comptaflow's or any third party's servers.

## 2. What the App accesses, and why

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Camera (rear) | Live collision-warning detection and dashcam recording |
| Camera (front) | Driver drowsiness / distraction monitoring |
| Microphone | Voice commands and dashcam audio track |
| Location | Turn-by-turn navigation, speed display, trip logging |
| Notifications | Parking-mode impact alerts |
| Storage (app-private) | Saving dashcam clips and trip history on your device |

## 3. Data storage

- Dashcam video clips, trip history, and incident logs are stored **only on your device**, in the App's private storage area.
- Deleting the App deletes this data. There is no cloud backup or account system in this version of the App.

## 4. Third-party services

- **Google Maps SDK** is used for navigation and map display, subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google ML Kit** performs on-device object and face detection; per Google's documentation this processing happens on-device and does not send images to Google servers for the on-device APIs used by this App.

## 5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves (e.g. if a future version adds optional cloud sync). Material changes will be reflected in the App's Play Store listing and this document's "Last updated" date.
